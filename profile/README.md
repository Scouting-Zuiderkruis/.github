## Welcome

Welcome to the GitHub organization of Scouting Zuiderkruis! We are a water scouting group from Hilversum, the Netherlands, with boats, sails and a clubhouse. Our main website is [zuiderkruis.nl](https://zuiderkruis.nl).

Welkom bij de GitHub-organisatie van Scouting Zuiderkruis. Hier staat de code van de websites en programma's die we voor onze eigen groep bouwen.

## What we build

- **Website:** [zuiderkruis.nl](https://zuiderkruis.nl), the home of our group.
- **Soos:** the site where members order drinks and food at our bar.
- **Acties:** where people sign up for our camps and pay online.

Most of our repositories are private. The public ones are on the [repositories tab](https://github.com/Scouting-Zuiderkruis?tab=repositories).
